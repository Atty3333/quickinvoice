Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we store

If you don't log in, your invoices stay only in your own browser (local storage). If you create an account, your email and the invoices you save are stored in Google Firebase so you can open them on any device.

Payments

Payments for Invoice Plus are handled by Gumroad. We never see your card details. Gumroad tells us your email so we can unlock Pro.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalised ads at Google Ad Settings or aboutads.info. Learn more at How Google uses data.

Your rights

You can delete saved invoices at any time. To delete your account and all its data, email us and we will do it within 30 days.
